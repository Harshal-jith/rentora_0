Private Residence Governance

Terms of Estate Residence

These Terms govern guest occupancy, estate access, staffing arrangements, and residence protocols across all properties in the Rentora Kerala portfolio. Booking a residence constitutes agreement to these terms.

Last Revised: January 1, 2026 • Standard Guest Code

01 Check-In & Estate Arrival Protocols

Standard check-in time across all private backwater and plantation estates begins at 14:00 (2:00 PM) IST, with check-out scheduled by 11:00 AM IST to allow deep sanitation and butler preparation. Early arrival or late departure must be pre-arranged with your assigned estate concierge. Government photo identification (Passport or Aadhar) is required for all adult guests prior to estate key handover.

02 Private Estate Conduct & House Etiquette

Our properties are serene sanctuaries situated in sensitive eco-zones and heritage precincts. Guests are expected to maintain appropriate conduct:

  • Noise & Quiet Hours: Outdoor amplified sound is restricted between 22:00 (10:00 PM) and 07:00 AM in accordance with local heritage zoning laws.
  • Occupancy Limits: The number of overnight residents must not exceed the maximum capacity specified in your booking confirmation. Unregistered overnight visitors are strictly prohibited.
  • Private Events: Gatherings, commercial photography, or events require prior written consent from the Rentora Executive Office.

03 Estate Staffing, Security & Butler Access

Every Rentora reservation includes dedicated on-site estate staff, including private chefs, housekeepers, and 24/7 security personnel. Staff quarters are designated on property grounds. Staff respect guest privacy at all times, with housekeeping scheduled at guest-preferred hours. Emergency maintenance personnel reserve access rights with prior notification to the head resident.

04 Reservation Amendments & Cancellation Policy

To maintain high availability for our estate owners, cancellations and rescheduling are structured as follows:

  • 30+ Days Prior: Full refund minus 5% administrative processing fee.
  • 15 to 29 Days Prior: 50% refund of total booking fee or full credit toward future stay within 12 months.
  • Under 14 Days: Booking fee is non-refundable; credit subject to estate owner approval.
  • Force Majeure: In cases of severe weather, monsoon alerts, or flight disruptions, stay dates can be rescheduled without penalty.

05 Security Deposits & Asset Care

A refundable security hold is authorized prior to arrival to cover incidental damages, specialized dining requests, or motorized watercraft rentals. The hold is fully released within 48 hours of check-out following joint inspection by the Estate Manager and guest. Guests are responsible for intentional damage to antique furnishings, artwork, or estate infrastructure.
