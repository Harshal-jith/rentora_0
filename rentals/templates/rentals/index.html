Everything you need to know about booking with Rentora Kerala.

How does renting a luxury estate in Kerala with Rentora work?

Browse our handpicked portfolio of luxury backwater mansions, cloud villas in Munnar, and coastal estates. Select your dates, and your private Kerala concierge will orchestrate your booking, airport chauffeur transfers from Cochin (COK) or Trivandrum (TRV), and private houseboat charters.

Are private chefs and authentic Ayurvedic treatments included?

Yes. Every Rentora Kerala property comes with dedicated staff and optional private master chefs specializing in fresh coastal Malabar cuisine. In-villa Ayurvedic doctors and certified therapists can also be arranged directly on your villa terrace.
