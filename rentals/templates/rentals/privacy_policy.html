RENTORA Privacy Policy

RENTORA (“RENTORA”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your Personal Data. This Privacy Policy explains how RENTORA collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use the RENTORA website, mobile application, platform, and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

RENTORA operates as a property rental platform that enables users to discover, inquire about, reserve, rent, and manage stays at properties listed on the Platform. Property owners, managers, or authorized representatives may also use the Platform to list and manage properties.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will obtain your consent or rely on another lawful basis permitted under applicable law before processing your Personal Data.

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable Indian data protection and privacy laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, 2023) and applicable rules and regulations thereunder.

Last Updated: September 5, 2026 • Version 3.0 (Global Edition)

01 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our services.

1.1 Information You Provide

Depending on how you use RENTORA, we may collect:

a) Account and Identification Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile / telephone number
  • Username and account credentials
  • Profile photograph, where you choose to provide one
  • Date of birth or age, where required for eligibility or verification
  • Government-issued identification information where required for verification, booking, safety, or legal compliance

b) Booking and Rental Information

  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests and occupants
  • Property preferences
  • Booking history and reservation details
  • Rental or stay-related communications
  • Special requests or requirements voluntarily provided by you

c) Payment and Transaction Information

  • Billing information, transaction details, payment status, and refund information
  • Limited payment-related information necessary to process or verify transactions

Payment card details may be processed directly by our payment service providers. RENTORA does not intend to retain complete card information unless necessary and legally permitted.

d) Property-Owner Information

If you list or manage a property on RENTORA, we may additionally collect:

  • Property ownership or authorization information
  • Property address and location
  • Property photographs, videos, descriptions, and amenities
  • Bank/payment details required for payouts
  • Tax or regulatory information where applicable
  • Government identification or business verification documents where required
  • Information relating to property management

e) Communications

  • Messages and inquiries sent through RENTORA
  • Communications with customer support
  • Feedback, reviews, complaints, and requests
  • Information you provide when contacting us by email, telephone, or other supported channels

Where permitted by law and appropriately disclosed, customer-support calls may be recorded for quality, security, training, and dispute-resolution purposes.

02 2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use RENTORA, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Approximate location information derived from technical data
  • Date and time of access, pages and properties viewed, search queries, and filters
  • Referring and exit pages, interaction with Platform features, error logs, and diagnostic information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

This information helps us operate, secure, analyze, and improve the Platform.

03 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

RENTORA may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies for the following purposes:

Strictly Necessary Technologies

Required for account login, authentication, security, bookings, payments, and core Platform functionality.

Functional Technologies

Used to remember preferences (such as light/dark mode and currency selections) and improve your experience.

Analytics Technologies

Used to understand how users interact with RENTORA and optimize Platform performance.

Marketing Technologies

Where legally permitted and, where required, with your consent, used to measure or personalize communications.

You may manage available cookie preferences through browser controls. Disabling certain cookies may affect some Platform functionality.

04 4. How RENTORA Uses Your Personal Data

We process your Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • a) Providing RENTORA Services: To create and manage your account, display properties, process inquiries, facilitate reservations, and provide rental-related services.
  • b) Processing Transactions: To facilitate payments, deposits, refunds, invoices, payouts, and other financial transactions.
  • c) Property and Booking Management: To communicate booking details between relevant users, property owners, property managers, and guests to fulfill reservations.
  • d) Identity and Property Verification: To verify users, property owners, properties, or transactions to prevent fraud and protect users.
  • e) Customer Support: To respond to questions, complaints, disputes, technical problems, and support requests.
  • f) Safety and Security: To protect users, properties, RENTORA, and the Platform against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • g) Legal and Regulatory Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, governmental requests, and tax obligations.
  • h) Platform Improvement: To analyze usage, troubleshoot problems, conduct research, and develop new features.
  • i) Personalization: To provide tailored property recommendations, search results, and features aligned with your preferences.
  • j) Transactional Communications: To send account verification messages, OTPs, booking confirmations, reservation updates, payment notifications, cancellation notices, and security alerts.
  • k) Marketing: Where permitted by applicable law and subject to your preferences, to send promotional communications about properties, services, and offers. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

05 5. Information About Other People

If you provide RENTORA with Personal Data belonging to another person—for example, a co-guest, occupant, employee, or representative—you confirm that you have the authority or permission required to provide that information. You should ensure that such individuals are appropriately informed about how their Personal Data may be processed.

06 6. Property Listings and Public Information

Information voluntarily submitted by property owners for publication on RENTORA may be displayed publicly or to other Platform users, including property name, location, photographs, videos, descriptions, amenities, pricing, availability, reviews, and host information. Property owners should not upload unnecessary Personal Data belonging to themselves, guests, tenants, or employees.

07 7. When We Share Personal Data

RENTORA does not sell your Personal Data merely because you use the Platform. We may share relevant Personal Data where reasonably necessary with:

  • a) Property Owners and Managers: Information necessary to facilitate inquiries, reservations, check-ins, and stay arrangements.
  • b) Service Providers: Third-party companies providing payment processing, cloud hosting, data storage, identity verification, customer support, analytics, email/SMS delivery, and fraud prevention under strict contractual safeguards.
  • c) Legal Authorities: Where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.
  • d) Security Partners: Where reasonably necessary to detect, prevent, or address fraud, security incidents, or unlawful activity.
  • e) Corporate Transactions: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of assets.
  • f) With Your Consent: Where you have specifically authorized or consented to a particular disclosure.

08 8. Third-Party Services and Links

The RENTORA Platform may contain links to third-party websites, applications, payment services, maps, or external platforms. Those third parties collect and process information independently of RENTORA under their own privacy policies. RENTORA is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that it does not operate or control.

09 9. Data Security

RENTORA takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or destruction. Security measures include access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, and secure infrastructure.

If RENTORA becomes aware of a Personal Data breach requiring notification under applicable law (including DPDP Act, 2023), we will take all legally required steps, including notifying relevant authorities and affected individuals.

10 10. Data Retention

RENTORA retains Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including fulfilling bookings, maintaining financial records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and complying with legal or tax obligations. When no longer required, RENTORA will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize the data.

11 11. International Data Transfers

RENTORA may use service providers or cloud infrastructure located outside India. Where Personal Data is transferred outside India, RENTORA will take appropriate measures required under applicable law to ensure data protection safeguards are enforced in accordance with legal requirements.

12 12. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about the Personal Data processed by RENTORA and how it is being used.
  • Correction: Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of Personal Data where permitted by applicable law.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent at any time without affecting lawful prior processing.
  • Grievance Redressal: Raise a complaint regarding the processing or handling of your Personal Data.
  • Nominate Another Individual: Exercise applicable rights through a validly appointed nominee in accordance with legal requirements.

13 13. How to Exercise Your Rights

To request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, please contact RENTORA using the details in the Grievance & Contact section below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests and will endeavor to respond within statutory timeframes.

14 14. Children's Privacy

RENTORA's services are intended for individuals who are legally capable of entering into rental agreements. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement where required by law.

15 15. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide to RENTORA is accurate and up to date. You should keep account credentials confidential, notify RENTORA of suspected unauthorized access, and avoid uploading fraudulent or unnecessary personal data.

16 16. Marketing Communications

Where applicable, RENTORA may send promotional communications via Email, SMS, WhatsApp, or Push notifications. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in the message. Essential transactional and booking notifications will continue to be delivered regardless of marketing opt-out status.

17 17. Reviews, Photos, and User Content

If you submit reviews, photographs, comments, or ratings to RENTORA, such content may be displayed to other users in accordance with our Terms of Service. Avoid including unnecessary personal data in publicly visible content.

18 18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RENTORA may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When material changes occur, we may provide notice through the Platform or via email. The updated policy becomes effective on the stated "Last Updated" date.
