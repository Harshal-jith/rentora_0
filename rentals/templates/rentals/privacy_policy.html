Privacy Policy

RENTORA (“RENTORA”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your Personal Data. This Privacy Policy explains how RENTORA collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you access or use the RENTORA website, mobile application, platform, and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

RENTORA operates as a property rental platform that enables users to discover, enquire about, reserve, rent, and manage stays at properties listed on the Platform. Property owners, managers, or authorised representatives may also use the Platform to list and manage properties.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will obtain your consent or rely on another lawful basis permitted under applicable law before processing your Personal Data. This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable Indian data protection and privacy laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and regulations thereunder.

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

01 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our services.

1.1 Information You Provide

Depending on how you use RENTORA, we may collect:

a) Account and identification information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile/telephone number
  • Username and account credentials
  • Profile photograph, where you choose to provide one
  • Date of birth or age, where required for eligibility or verification
  • Government-issued identification information where required for verification, booking, safety, or legal compliance

b) Booking and rental information

  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests/occupants
  • Property preferences
  • Booking history
  • Reservation details
  • Rental or stay-related communications
  • Special requests or requirements voluntarily provided by you

c) Payment and transaction information

  • Billing information
  • Transaction details
  • Payment status
  • Refund information
  • Limited payment-related information necessary to process or verify transactions

Payment card details may be processed directly by our payment service providers. RENTORA does not intend to retain complete card information unless necessary and legally permitted.

d) Property-owner information

If you list or manage a property on RENTORA, we may additionally collect:

  • Property ownership or authorisation information
  • Property address and location
  • Property photographs and videos
  • Property descriptions and amenities
  • Bank/payment details required for payouts
  • Tax or regulatory information where applicable
  • Government identification or business verification documents where required
  • Information relating to property management

e) Communications

  • Messages and enquiries sent through RENTORA
  • Communications with customer support
  • Feedback, reviews, complaints, and requests
  • Information you provide when contacting us by email, telephone, or other supported channels

Where permitted by law and appropriately disclosed, customer-support calls may be recorded for quality, security, training, and dispute-resolution purposes.

02 2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use RENTORA, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location information derived from technical data
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages and properties viewed
  • Search queries and filters
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Interaction with features of the Platform
  • Error logs and diagnostic information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

This information helps us operate, secure, analyse, and improve the Platform.

03 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

RENTORA may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies. These technologies may be used for:

  • Strictly Necessary Technologies: Required for account login, authentication, security, bookings, payments, and core Platform functionality.
  • Functional Technologies: Used to remember preferences and improve your experience.
  • Analytics Technologies: Used to understand how users interact with RENTORA and improve our Platform.
  • Marketing Technologies: Where legally permitted and, where required, with your consent, these technologies may be used to measure or personalise advertising and marketing communications.

You may manage available cookie preferences through the cookie controls provided on the Platform. Disabling certain cookies may affect some Platform functionality.

04 4. How RENTORA Uses Your Personal Data

We may process your Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • a) Providing RENTORA services: To create and manage your account, display properties, process enquiries, facilitate reservations, and provide rental-related services.
  • b) Processing transactions: To facilitate payments, deposits, refunds, invoices, payouts, and other financial transactions associated with the Platform.
  • c) Property and booking management: To communicate booking details between relevant users, property owners, property managers, and guests where necessary to fulfil a reservation or rental arrangement.
  • d) Identity and property verification: To verify users, property owners, properties, or transactions where necessary to prevent fraud, protect users, or comply with legal requirements.
  • e) Customer support: To respond to questions, complaints, disputes, technical problems, and other support requests.
  • f) Safety and security: To protect users, properties, RENTORA, and the Platform against fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, and other security threats.
  • g) Legal and regulatory compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, governmental requests, taxation requirements, or other legal obligations.
  • h) Platform improvement: To analyse usage, troubleshoot problems, conduct research, develop new features, and improve RENTORA's products and services.
  • i) Personalisation: To provide property recommendations, search results, content, and features that may be relevant to your preferences and interactions with RENTORA.
  • j) Communications: To send transactional and service-related communications (Account verification, OTPs, Booking confirmations, Reservation updates, Payment notifications, Cancellation/refund notifications, Security alerts).
  • k) Marketing: Where permitted by applicable law and subject to your preferences, RENTORA may send promotional communications about properties, services, offers, features, or other relevant information. You may opt out of marketing communications without affecting essential transactional or service-related communications.

05 5. Information About Other People

If you provide RENTORA with Personal Data belonging to another person—for example, a co-guest, occupant, employee, or representative—you confirm that you have the authority or permission required to provide that information. You should ensure that such individuals are appropriately informed about how their Personal Data may be processed.

06 6. Property Listings and Public Information

Information voluntarily submitted by property owners for publication on RENTORA may be displayed publicly or to other Platform users. This may include:

  • Property name & general location
  • Property photographs and videos
  • Property descriptions, amenities & availability
  • Pricing, reviews, and ratings
  • Host/property-manager information where applicable

Property owners should not upload unnecessary Personal Data belonging to themselves, guests, tenants, employees, or other individuals.

07 7. When We Share Personal Data

RENTORA does not sell your Personal Data merely because you use the Platform. We may share relevant Personal Data where reasonably necessary with:

  • a) Property owners and property managers: Information necessary to facilitate enquiries, reservations, check-ins, rental arrangements, and related services.
  • b) Service providers: Payment processing, cloud hosting, data storage, identity verification, customer support, analytics, communications, email/SMS delivery, fraud prevention, and security partners.
  • c) Legal and governmental authorities: Where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.
  • d) Fraud prevention and security partners: Where reasonably necessary to detect, prevent, investigate, or address fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity.
  • e) Corporate transactions: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction.
  • f) With your consent: Where you have specifically authorised or consented to a particular disclosure.

08 8. Third-Party Services and Links

The RENTORA Platform may contain links to third-party websites, applications, payment services, maps, communication services, or other external platforms. Those third parties may collect and process information independently of RENTORA. Their collection and use of Personal Data will be governed by their own privacy policies and terms. RENTORA is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that it does not operate or control.

09 9. Data Security

RENTORA takes reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or destruction. Security measures may include appropriate access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, secure infrastructure, and internal security procedures.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You acknowledge that transmission of information over the internet carries inherent risks.

If RENTORA becomes aware of a Personal Data breach requiring notification under applicable law, we will take the steps required by law, including notifying relevant authorities and/or affected individuals where applicable.

10 10. Data Retention

RENTORA retains Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including to provide and maintain our services, complete transactions, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal or reporting requirements.

When Personal Data is no longer required, RENTORA will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise, or dispose of it in accordance with applicable law. Deleting your account does not necessarily mean every record will be immediately deleted where retention is legally required or reasonably necessary.

11 11. International Data Transfers

RENTORA may use service providers or infrastructure located outside India. Where Personal Data is transferred outside India, RENTORA will take appropriate measures required under applicable law to protect the Personal Data and ensure that such transfer is carried out in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

12 12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your Personal Data, including:

  • Access: Request information about Personal Data processed by RENTORA.
  • Correction: Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of Personal Data where permitted by applicable law.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Grievance redressal: Raise a complaint regarding the handling of your Personal Data.
  • Nominate another individual: Exercise applicable rights through a validly appointed nominee.

13 13. How to Exercise Your Rights

To request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or other privacy-related assistance, please contact RENTORA using the contact details provided in the Grievance & Contact section below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests. RENTORA will endeavour to respond to valid requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

14 14. Children's Privacy

RENTORA's services are intended for individuals who are legally capable of entering into the relevant rental or service arrangement. We do not knowingly seek to collect Personal Data from children except where permitted or required by applicable law and with appropriate parental or guardian involvement where required. If you believe that a child has provided Personal Data to RENTORA without authorisation, please contact us immediately.

15 15. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide to RENTORA is accurate and up to date. You should also:

  • Keep your account credentials confidential.
  • Notify RENTORA of suspected unauthorised account access.
  • Avoid uploading unnecessary Personal Data belonging to other individuals.
  • Not provide false, misleading, or fraudulent information.
  • Use the Platform in accordance with RENTORA's Terms and applicable law.

16 16. Marketing Communications

Where applicable, RENTORA may send promotional communications through Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push notifications, or In-platform notifications. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the relevant communication or through account settings. Even if you opt out of marketing communications, RENTORA may continue to send essential service communications relating to your account, security, transactions, reservations, payments, or other services you have requested.

17 17. Reviews, Photos and User Content

If you submit reviews, photographs, comments, ratings, or other content to RENTORA, such content may be displayed to other users in accordance with our Terms of Service. You should avoid including unnecessary Personal Data about yourself or another individual in publicly visible content. Where required, RENTORA may moderate, remove, or restrict content that violates applicable law, our policies, or the rights of others.

18 18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RENTORA may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices. When we make material changes, we may provide notice through the Platform, email, or another appropriate communication method. The updated Privacy Policy will become effective from the date stated at the beginning of the updated policy.
