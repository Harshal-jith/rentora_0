RENTORA Privacy Policy

﻿RENTORA Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

RENTORA (“RENTORA”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your Personal Data.

This Privacy Policy explains how RENTORA collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you access or use the RENTORA website, application, platform, and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

RENTORA operates as a property rental platform that enables users to discover, enquire about, reserve, rent, and manage stays at properties listed on the Platform. Property owners, property managers, hosts, or authorised representatives may also use the Platform to list and manage properties.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, RENTORA will obtain your consent before processing your Personal Data or process it on another lawful basis permitted under applicable law.

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable Indian data protection and privacy laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and applicable rules and regulations thereunder.

1 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our services.

The information we collect depends on how you interact with RENTORA.

1.1 Information You Provide

Depending on how you use RENTORA, we may collect the following information.

a) Account and Identification Information

Full name

Email address

Mobile or telephone number

Username and account credentials

Profile photograph, where you choose to provide one

Date of birth or age, where required for eligibility, verification, or legal compliance

Government-issued identification information where required for verification, booking, safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance

b) Booking and Rental Information

When you search for, enquire about, or reserve a property, we may collect:

Check-in and check-out dates

Number of guests or occupants

Property preferences

Booking history and reservation details

Rental or stay-related communications

Special requests or requirements voluntarily provided by you

Cancellation and refund information

c) Payment and Transaction Information

We may collect or receive:

Billing information

Transaction details

Payment status

Refund information

Payout information, where applicable

Limited payment-related information necessary to process or verify transactions

Payment card and other payment information may be processed by third-party payment service providers. Where applicable, such providers may process your information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

d) Property Owner and Host Information

If you list or manage a property through RENTORA, we may additionally collect:

Property ownership or authorisation information

Property address and location

Property photographs and videos

Property descriptions and amenities

Pricing and availability information

Bank or payment details required for payouts

Tax or regulatory information where applicable

Government identification or business verification documents where required

Information relating to property management

e) Communications

We may collect information contained in communications you send through or to RENTORA, including:

Property enquiries

Messages

Customer-support communications

Feedback

Reviews

Complaints

Requests

Other information you provide when contacting us

Where permitted by applicable law and appropriately disclosed, customer-support calls may be recorded for quality assurance, security, training, and dispute-resolution purposes.

IP address

Browser type and version

Device type

Operating system

Device identifiers

Approximate location information derived from technical data

Date and time of access

Pages and properties viewed

Search queries and filters

Referring and exit pages

Interactions with Platform features

Error logs and diagnostic information

Cookies and similar technologies

This information helps us operate, secure, analyse, troubleshoot, and improve the Platform.

3 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

RENTORA may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies.

These technologies may be used for the following purposes:

Strictly Necessary Technologies

These technologies are required for essential Platform functions such as account login, authentication, security, bookings, payments, and other core functionality.

Functional Technologies

These technologies help us remember preferences, such as display preferences or currency selections, and improve your experience.

Analytics Technologies

These technologies help us understand how users interact with RENTORA and improve Platform performance and functionality.

Marketing Technologies

Where legally permitted and, where required, with your consent, these technologies may be used to measure or personalise marketing communications.

You may manage available cookie preferences through your browser settings or any cookie controls provided by RENTORA.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the Platform.

4 4. How RENTORA Uses Your Personal Data

We may process your Personal Data for the following purposes:

a) Providing RENTORA Services

To create and manage your account, display properties, process enquiries, facilitate reservations, and provide rental-related services.

b) Processing Transactions

To facilitate payments, deposits, refunds, invoices, payouts, and other financial transactions associated with the Platform.

c) Property and Booking Management

To communicate relevant booking and rental information between guests, renters, property owners, hosts, property managers, and other relevant parties where necessary to fulfil a reservation or rental arrangement.

d) Identity and Property Verification

To verify users, property owners, properties, or transactions where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or the proper operation of RENTORA.

e) Customer Support

To respond to questions, complaints, disputes, technical problems, and other support requests.

f) Safety and Security

To protect users, properties, RENTORA, and the Platform against fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, security incidents, and other harmful or unlawful activity.

g) Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, governmental requests, tax obligations, and other legal requirements.

h) Platform Improvement

To analyse usage, troubleshoot technical issues, conduct research, develop new features, and improve RENTORA's products and services.

i) Personalisation

To provide relevant property recommendations, search results, content, and features based on your preferences and interactions with RENTORA.

j) Transactional Communications

To send account verification messages, one-time passwords (OTPs), booking confirmations, reservation updates, payment notifications, cancellation and refund notifications, security alerts, and other service-related communications.

k) Marketing

Where permitted by applicable law and subject to your preferences, RENTORA may send promotional communications regarding properties, services, features, offers, and other relevant information.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

We may share Personal Data with third-party service providers that assist us with services such as:

Payment processing

Data storage

Identity verification

Customer support

Analytics

Email and SMS delivery

Fraud prevention

Security

Software and technology infrastructure

Such service providers may process Personal Data as necessary to provide services to RENTORA and subject to applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

c) Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose Personal Data where required or permitted by applicable law, regulation, legal process, court order, or valid governmental request.

d) Fraud and Security

We may share relevant information where reasonably necessary to detect, prevent, investigate, or address fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity.

e) Business Transfers

Personal Data may be transferred or disclosed as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, subject to applicable law.

RENTORA takes reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or destruction.

RENTORA retains Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

Providing and maintaining our services

Completing transactions

Maintaining account and business records

Maintaining financial and tax records

Resolving disputes

Preventing fraud and abuse

Enforcing our agreements

Complying with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, or reporting requirements
