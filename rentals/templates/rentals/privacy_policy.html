Guest Protection Architecture

Privacy & Data Security Charter

At Rentora Kerala, discretion, privacy, and absolute confidentiality form the bedrock of our guest relations. This policy details how we safeguard your personal data across our private estate bookings and digital concierge service.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 2.4 (Global Edition)

01 Guest Confidentiality & High-Discretion Protocol

We recognize that guests entrusting us with their private stays expect complete confidentiality. Rentora maintains strict non-disclosure procedures across all private villas, island retreats, and backwater estates. Personal guest identities, travel itineraries, companion lists, and arrival methods are handled under strict confidentiality protocols and are accessible only to essential concierge personnel.

02 Data Collection & Secure Processing

To facilitate seamless estate reservations and bespoke concierge services, we collect minimal necessary information, including:

  • Identity Verification: Full name, passport/government photo identity, contact phone, and verified email address.
  • Reservation Requirements: Preferred travel dates, guest count, dietary preferences, special health accessibility needs, and customized butler requests.
  • Technical Metadata: Encrypted IP addresses, device identifiers, and browser session parameters logged strictly for security monitoring and fraud prevention.

03 Concierge & Third-Party Sharing Boundaries

Rentora does not sell, rent, or commercialize guest personal data to advertising networks or third-party marketers under any circumstances. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Private Estate Hosts & Butler Staff: Essential arrival schedules and guest preferences required for in-residence staffing.
  • Charter Partners: Yacht captains, helicopter pilots, or private drivers explicitly contracted by you through our concierge desk.
  • Legal Authorities: Statutory disclosures required under applicable Indian federal law, maritime safety regulations, or court orders.

04 Cookies, Theme & Currency Preferences

Our digital portal utilizes functional session cookies and browser local storage strictly to remember your lighting preferences (By Day / By Night) and currency display choices (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED). These technical controls ensure seamless page transitions without storing personally identifiable information in your browser environment.

05 Guest Data Sovereignty & Rights

You retain full authority over your personal information. Registered members may view, update, or edit their details anytime through their Member Profile. You may also request a full copy of your stored records or request complete deletion of your account history by contacting our Data Protection Officer at project.propertyrentals@gmail.com.

Questions regarding your privacy?

Our dedicated Privacy & Legal Desk in Fort Kochi is available 24/7.
